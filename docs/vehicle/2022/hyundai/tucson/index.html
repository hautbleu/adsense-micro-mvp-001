2022 Hyundai Tucson Safety Overview

This prototype summarizes NHTSA safety records for the 2022 Hyundai Tucson while preserving the distinction between consumer reports and confirmed recalls.

Snapshot

The dataset contains 383 unique complaint reference numbers represented by 601 component rows. It also contains 3 unique recall campaigns represented by 7 component rows.

154 complaint references have more than one component classification; the largest has 3. This is why raw row counts must not be presented as complaint counts.

Data notes

Source: NHTSA bulk safety datasets. Consumer complaints are reports submitted to NHTSA and are not, by themselves, verified defects. A complaint can be classified under multiple components, so complaint totals on this site deduplicate NHTSA ODINO reference numbers.

Complaint scope in this prototype: reports received during 2020–2026. Recall data uses the NHTSA post-2010 recall dataset. This prototype is not affiliated with Hyundai or NHTSA and is not a substitute for a VIN-specific recall check.
