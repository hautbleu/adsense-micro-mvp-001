Data notes

Source: NHTSA bulk safety datasets. Consumer complaints are reports submitted to NHTSA and are not, by themselves, verified defects. Complaint totals deduplicate NHTSA ODINO reference numbers.

Complaint scope in this prototype: reports received during 2020–2026. Recall data uses the NHTSA post-2010 recall dataset. This prototype is not affiliated with the manufacturer or NHTSA and is not a substitute for a VIN-specific recall check.
