2022 Hyundai Tucson NHTSA Complaints

NHTSA complaint records in this prototype contain 383 unique ODINO references across 601 component rows.

Why the counts differ

NHTSA permits the same complaint reference to appear once for each component classification. In this vehicle slice, 154 complaint references are multi-component, with a maximum of 3 classifications on one reference. Unique complaint totals therefore use ODINO rather than row count.

Crash and fire flags

25 unique complaint references contain a crash flag and 4 contain a fire flag. A flag reports what was submitted with the complaint; it does not establish that a vehicle defect caused the event.

Data notes

Source: NHTSA bulk safety datasets. Consumer complaints are reports submitted to NHTSA and are not, by themselves, verified defects. A complaint can be classified under multiple components, so complaint totals on this site deduplicate NHTSA ODINO reference numbers.

Complaint scope in this prototype: reports received during 2020–2026. Recall data uses the NHTSA post-2010 recall dataset. This prototype is not affiliated with Hyundai or NHTSA and is not a substitute for a VIN-specific recall check.
